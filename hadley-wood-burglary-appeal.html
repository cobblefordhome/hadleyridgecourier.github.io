Police appeal after masked men force their way into Hadley Wood home

Two masked men forced the rear patio doors of a detached house in Larkspur Avenue on Friday afternoon and took a necklace and a watch, police said.
By Courier newsdesk · Published 07:30, Saturday 14 November 2026 · Updated 12:15
UPDATE 12:15, Saturday 14 November: a 24-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of conspiracy to commit burglary and remains in custody. Police have also released a description of the stolen items: a diamond-style necklace and a gold-coloured watch, serial number HR-M-04417. Anyone offered items matching this description is asked to contact police.
Police are appealing for witnesses. (Illustration)

Police are appealing for witnesses after two masked men forced their way into a house in Larkspur Avenue, Hadley Wood, at about 4.10pm on Friday 13 November.

Officers said a woman in her 50s, who works at the address, was restrained with cable ties, and that one of the men displayed what appeared to be a handgun. A second woman, in her 30s, was in an upstairs room at the time.

The men took a necklace and a watch before leaving at about 4.35pm. Neighbours reported seeing two people running towards a grey panel van, which was driven away. Neither woman was physically injured.

Detective Constable Ellis Marr said: “This was a frightening experience for the two women in the house. We are keen to hear from anyone who was in the area on Friday afternoon, particularly drivers with dashcam footage or anyone who noticed a grey panel van waiting nearby.”

Police are treating the incident as an aggravated burglary. Enquiries are continuing.

Contact: anyone with information is asked to contact police quoting reference CAD 5521/13NOV. Information can also be given anonymously through an independent charity.
